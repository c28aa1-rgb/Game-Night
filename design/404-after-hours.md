# After Hours

Play is expressed through the relationship between solid forms and the spaces they leave behind. A missing shape can carry as much meaning as a complete one. Meticulously crafted edges, measured offsets, and generous negative space make absence feel intentional; the composition invites a return rather than declaring an ending.

Color takes its cues from lacquered game pieces resting on a dark felt table. Table teal (#123D39) and shadow teal (#0C2C29) hold warm ivory (#FFF4DC), butter (#F2D481), coral (#F28C78), and lilac (#B9ACD8). The product of deep expertise should be visible in the restraint of these relationships: luminous objects against a rich, quiet field, without decorative gradients competing for attention.

Scale establishes a rhythm of recognition, interruption, and recognition. Repeated geometry is interrupted once, with painstaking attention to the optical weight of the empty interval. Fine concentric outlines suggest an object's former position while a displaced token hints that the pattern can be completed again. This is the quiet reference to a game waiting for its missing piece.

Typography is sparse and purposeful. Bricolage Grotesque supplies broad, distinctive display forms; Instrument Sans keeps explanation and actions plain. The artwork uses geometric numeral forms rather than paragraphs. Master-level execution lives in the calibration of margins, soft shadows, and the smallest alignment, not in the number of embellishments.

The composition should feel labored over with care, then edited until only the essential remains. One sculptural arrangement carries the personality; its surrounding space provides calm and direction. Every detail earns its place through clarity or balance.

## Page plan

- Audience: Game Night players arriving at a missing URL on a phone or TV.
- Job: explain that the page cannot be found and return the player to the game selection.
- Layout: a quiet brand header, left-aligned copy and actions, a large original tile composition on the right, then a small footer. On phones the illustration sits above the copy.
- Signature: two solid number-four tiles and one empty circular socket, with the displaced token beneath them.
- Critique: a huge standalone 404 on a bordered card would repeat the old page. The new composition turns the error into physical game pieces and removes the card container.
- Accessibility: native links, visible focus, descriptive heading, decorative image with empty alt, no required animation or JavaScript, no fixed-height clipping.
