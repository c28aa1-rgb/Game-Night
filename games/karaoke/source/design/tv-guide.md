# Signal Blue

Clarity begins with a familiar object enlarged until its purpose becomes unmistakable. The keycap, the screen, and the connecting line carry meaning before language arrives. Space around each form is deliberately generous; every edge and interval should feel meticulously crafted.

Royal blue creates continuity, soft white carries instruction, and a single pink highlight identifies the next action. Yellow marks progress rather than decoration. This restrained color grammar is the product of deep expertise: one clear emphasis at a time, with no competing ornaments.

Scale follows the hand and the eye. A physical key becomes a small stage; a system menu becomes a carefully framed specimen. Captions remain sparse, while familiar controls retain their recognizable proportions. Painstaking attention to legibility matters more than surface novelty.

Motion shows cause and effect. A key depresses, a panel arrives, a television accepts the connection. Each movement resolves before the next begins. Master-level execution means refining these relationships until the sequence can be understood with almost no reading.

## Application

- Existing Blue Screen theme: Royal #1A2AA8, Deep #0E1670, Ink #0A0C3A, Lyric #FF3EA5, Progress #FFE24A, Paper #F6F4FF.
- Display: Dela Gothic One. Body: Instrument Sans. Utility: VT323. System menu examples use familiar system typography.
- Signature: oversized animated Windows + K keycaps, followed by a recognizable Cast-panel example.
- One task per visual step; device and connection choices stay in the same place.
- Illustrations are original, labelled examples, not captures of the visitor's actual device.
- Motion: 140 ms press, 280 ms content change, 360 ms dialog entrance; decelerating entrance and accelerating exit. Reduced motion removes travel and keypress animation.
- Canvas preview: tv-guide-preview.png. Production PNG illustrations live in source/public/tv-guide/.
