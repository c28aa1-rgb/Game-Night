Hues&Cues

One word. Four hundred and eighty colours.

2–10 players · one TV · one phone each

Start here TV screen The board, all 480 squares of it, plus everybody's pins as they land. Put it on the big screen — this is the only thing the room looks at. → On your phone Player Join with the room code and pick your colour. Scanning the code on the TV does the same thing. →

How it goes

On your turn the game deals you four secret colours. Keep one, then say a single word out loud — no more. Everybody else pins the square they think you mean. Say a second word, they pin again. Land on the exact square for three points, one of the eight touching it for two, the ring beyond that for one. You score for every player you led home.
