# Common Ground

Common Ground treats agreement as a physical force: many small forms lean, collect, and settle into a shared center while a single counterform holds deliberate distance. Space does not merely contain the work; it measures belonging. Wide pasture-like fields give clustered marks room to become meaningful, and every gap is tuned with the precision of a master composition.

Color behaves like material gathered after dusk. Deep mineral blue carries the weight of painted timber, warm white has the softness of milk glass, and yellow flashes like fairground bulbs against weathered red and pasture green. One vivid pink interrupts the system without overwhelming it. Every relationship is meticulously calibrated so contrast feels inevitable rather than applied.

Scale turns repetition into evidence. Modest tags, dots, rails, and punched circles accumulate patiently until they read as one visual body; then a lone shape, enlarged only by its isolation, changes the balance. Rhythm should feel handmade yet exact, the result of countless refinements by someone with deep command of pattern and pause.

Composition follows the logic of a pen: boundaries are clear, entrances are generous, and movement bends toward the center. Geometry may carry softened corners and clipped edges, but never becomes cute or careless. The work must retain the tension of an expertly built object, with every alignment and interval showing painstaking attention.

Typography appears as a rare sign painted onto the structure, never as explanation. Bold, compact lettering may anchor a field while small utility marks record sequence and measure. The final expression should feel labored over with care, visually communicative before it is read, and executed at the level of a crafted artifact meant to endure close inspection.
