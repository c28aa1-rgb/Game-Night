# Tropical Optical Camouflage

## Palette

- **Night hide** `#070608` — primary background.
- **Charcoal scale** `#151319` — raised surfaces.
- **Chalk flash** `#F5F0D8` — primary text and high-contrast marks.
- **Fuchsia tell** `#EF2F70` — danger, Chameleon, and first spectral stop.
- **Mango clue** `#FF8A1E` — active speaker and interaction.
- **Pollen code** `#F6D447` — timers, room code, and target reveal.
- **Leaf signal** `#98C93C` — confirmation and Town scoring.
- **Lagoon trace** `#24B8B4` — selections and information.
- **Violet shadow** `#8254C7` — depth and final spectral stop.

## Type

- **Display:** `Arial Black`, `Arial Narrow`, sans-serif — compressed, poster-like, used only for titles and decisive results.
- **Body:** `Trebuchet MS`, `Aptos`, sans-serif — warm, legible, and slightly humanist.
- **Utility:** `Cascadia Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace — room codes, timers, coordinates, and phase labels.

## Layout

The TV is a social instrument: a low header, a dominant square grid, and one contextual rail. The phone is a private card: identity at the top, one decisive action in the thumb zone, and no secret information below the fold. The 5×5 grid is the shared spatial truth across both surfaces.

## Signature

The grid begins as one camouflage field. Phase changes separate the cells with a spectral wave; the reveal collapses that spectrum onto the target word while surrounding cells recede. This is the only theatrical flourish. Everything else remains crisp, tactile, and calm.

## Motion identity

- Personality: energetic with controlled overshoot.
- Signature easing: `cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)`.
- Durations: 140ms quick, 280ms standard, 720ms reveal.
- Entrance: 14px upward travel plus opacity and a subtle scale settle.
- Reduced motion: remove travel, scale, stagger, and ambient drift; preserve instant state/color changes.
